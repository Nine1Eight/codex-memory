# Technical Invention Record

## Purpose

This document is a structured invention record for patent-counsel development. It is intended to support claim drafting, inventor interview preparation, evidence collection, and disclosure normalization.

## 1. Full System And Process Architecture

Document the end-to-end system architecture, major subsystems, data or material flow, control points, and the complete operational sequence from setup through output, disposal, or reset.

## 2. Candidate Novelty Center

State the narrowest technically defensible novelty nucleus. Describe what appears to be new, why it is central, and how it differs from routine implementation choices or known combinations.

## 3. Ownership And Disclosure Chain

Record inventorship inputs, dates, locations of conception and reduction-to-practice evidence, internal disclosures, external disclosures, and any third-party dependencies that could affect title or priority posture.

## 4. Biological And Chemical Boundaries

Define the biological, chemical, environmental, and process boundaries of the system. Specify what materials, concentrations, reaction windows, handling conditions, and exclusions are within scope and which are intentionally out of scope.

## 5. Binder And Cartridge Architecture

Describe the binder design, cartridge structure, physical interfaces, retention features, channeling or flow paths, replaceable elements, and any modular interactions between consumable and reusable components.

## 6. Independent Concept Set

Capture independent claim-oriented concepts at three levels:

- System concept
- Method concept
- Cartridge or article concept

Each concept should be framed in technology terms rather than legal conclusion terms.

## 7. Dependent Fallback Positions

List narrower embodiments, optional constraints, preferred ranges, alternative materials, geometric variants, sequence variants, control logic refinements, and safety subfeatures that can support layered fallback claim positions.

## 8. Prior-Art Pressure Map

Map the areas most likely to face prior-art pressure. Separate likely crowded elements from less crowded integration points. Note where novelty may reside in arrangement, coupling, boundary conditions, or operational sequence rather than isolated components.

## 9. Evidence-To-Claim Matrix

For each core inventive concept, identify the best available supporting evidence:

- Lab notes
- Drawings
- CAD or schematics
- Bench data
- Simulation data
- Photographs
- Prototype logs
- Source files
- Revision history
- Witness or corroboration sources

## 10. Safety And Containment Barriers

Document all barriers that reduce misuse, leakage, contamination, failure propagation, unsafe exposure, or improper deployment. Include passive and active safeguards, fault states, and shutdown or isolation behavior where relevant.

## 11. Patent-Versus-Trade-Secret Strategy

Separate features that should likely be disclosed and claimed from features that may be better retained as trade secrets. Focus on detectability, reverse-engineerability, enforcement value, manufacturing know-how, and business dependence.

## 12. Source Record And USPTO Defensibility Standards

Identify the evidentiary standards required to support defensible patent development:

- Dated records
- Author attribution
- Version history
- Stable file provenance
- Experiment traceability
- Clear linkage between evidence and claimed subject matter
- Preservation of original source artifacts

## 13. Open Items For Counsel

Track unresolved issues that should be answered before formal drafting:

- Claim breadth targets
- Jurisdiction priorities
- Public disclosure risks
- Third-party rights questions
- Inventorship edge cases
- Enablement support gaps

## 14. Disclaimer

This is a technical invention record for patent-counsel development, not a filed patent and not a patentability, validity, infringement, or freedom-to-operate opinion.
